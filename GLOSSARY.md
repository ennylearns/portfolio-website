# Personal Website

A personal teaching and builder site focused on how practical AI improves daily life, small businesses, and the economy in Nigeria and across Africa.

## Language

**Reader**:
An African founder, small-business operator, or ecosystem decision-maker visiting the site to learn how AI creates practical value.
_Avoid_: User, visitor, audience, customer

**Subscriber**:
A **Reader** who opted into the no-schedule email list to be notified when new **Work** is published.
_Avoid_: Lead, follower, member

**Work**:
A published **Playbook**, **Prototype**, or **Essay** created by Eniola Odunaiya.
_Avoid_: Post, content, item, entry, piece

**Playbook**:
A step-by-step practical guide showing how to apply AI to a specific Nigerian or African daily-life or small-business problem.
_Avoid_: Tutorial, blog post, how-to

**Prototype**:
A working AI tool, demo, or skill built by the author to solve a concrete problem, spanning either general AI craft or Nigeria/Africa-specific workflows.
_Avoid_: Project, side project, app

**Essay**:
A long-form analysis or argument exploring how AI affects the Nigerian and broader African economy.
_Avoid_: Article, opinion piece, blog post

**Library**:
The collection of all published **Playbooks** and **Essays** on the site, organized by **Theme**.
_Avoid_: Blog, feed, writing

**Theme**:
One of three fixed topic pillars in the **Library**: `Daily Life`, `Business`, or `Economy`.
_Avoid_: Tag, category, topic
